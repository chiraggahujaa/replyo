# BrightSmile Dental — Pricing Guide

All prices are in INR (₹) and are indicative starting prices. Final costs are
confirmed after an in-person consultation.

## Consultations & Diagnostics
- New patient consultation: ₹500 (waived if you proceed with treatment the same day)
- Routine check-up: ₹400
- Dental X-ray (single): ₹300
- Full-mouth X-ray (OPG): ₹800

## Cleaning & Hygiene
- Scaling & polishing (standard cleaning): ₹1,500
- Deep cleaning (per quadrant): ₹2,500
- Fluoride treatment: ₹800

## Cosmetic
- Teeth whitening (in-clinic, single session): ₹8,000
- Take-home whitening kit: ₹5,000
- Composite veneer (per tooth): ₹4,000
- Porcelain veneer (per tooth): ₹12,000

## Restorative
- Composite filling: ₹1,000–2,500 depending on size
- Root canal treatment (front tooth): ₹4,000
- Root canal treatment (molar): ₹7,000
- Dental crown (porcelain/ceramic): ₹9,000
- Dental implant (single, including crown): ₹35,000

## Orthodontics
- Braces (metal, full treatment): from ₹40,000
- Invisalign (full treatment): from ₹1,50,000

No-cost EMI and payment plans are available for treatments above ₹20,000.
Ask our team for details.
