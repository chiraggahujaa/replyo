# Riverbend Wellness Spa — Services

Every service is customised on the day. Not sure what to book? Call us and we'll help you
choose, or start with a 60-minute Signature Massage and tell your therapist your goals.

## Massage Therapy

- **Riverbend Signature Massage** — our most popular. A fully customised massage blending
  Swedish and light deep-tissue work, tailored to your pressure preference. *(60 or 90 min)*
- **Deep Tissue Massage** — firm, focused work for chronic tension and tight muscles. Best
  for knots in the shoulders, neck and lower back. *(60 or 90 min)*
- **Hot Stone Massage** — warm basalt stones melt deep tension and improve circulation.
  Deeply relaxing. *(75 min)*
- **Prenatal Massage** — gentle, side-lying massage for expectant guests in their second or
  third trimester, performed by a therapist certified in prenatal work. *(60 min)*
- **Sports & Recovery Massage** — targeted work for active bodies: stretching, trigger-point
  release, and recovery-focused techniques. *(60 or 90 min)*
- **Couples Massage** — two therapists, two tables, side by side in our couples suite. A
  lovely way to share the experience. *(60 min)*

## Facials & Skincare

- **Classic Facial** — a relaxing cleanse, exfoliation, extraction, mask and massage,
  customised to your skin type. Great for a first facial. *(60 min)*
- **HydraFacial** — our signature results-driven treatment: deep cleansing, gentle
  exfoliation and hydrating serums for an immediate glow. *(60 min)*
- **Anti-Ageing Facial** — targets fine lines and dullness with peptides, vitamin C and a
  firming massage. *(75 min)*
- **Express Facial** — a quick refresh: cleanse, exfoliate, mask. Perfect on a lunch break
  or added before a massage. *(30 min)*
- **Back ("Bacial") Treatment** — a deep-cleansing facial for the back; ideal before events
  or beach season. *(45 min)*

## Body Treatments

- **Detox Body Wrap** — a warm mineral or seaweed wrap to soften skin and unwind. *(60 min)*
- **Sea Salt Body Scrub** — full-body exfoliation that leaves skin polished and smooth.
  *(45 min)*

## Enhancements (add to any service)

- Aromatherapy (choose your blend)
- CBD balm upgrade
- Hot stone add-on
- Scalp & hair oil treatment
- Extended massage time (extra 30 min)

If you have a specific concern — an old injury, sensitive skin, pregnancy, or a fragrance
allergy — tell us when you book and we'll adapt the service. We do not currently offer
medical/injectable "med-spa" services.
