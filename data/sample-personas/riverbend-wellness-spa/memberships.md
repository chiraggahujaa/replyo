# Riverbend Wellness Spa — Memberships

Memberships are the easiest way to make self-care a habit — and the best value we offer.
Each month you receive a service credit plus member pricing on everything else.

## The plans

### Essential — ₹4,700 / month
- One 60-minute service each month: **Signature Massage** or **Classic Facial**
- 10% off all additional services and retail
- Bring-a-friend guest rate

### Signature — ₹7,400 / month
- One premium 60–75 minute service each month: **Deep Tissue**, **Hot Stone**,
  **HydraFacial**, or **Anti-Ageing Facial**
- 15% off all additional services and retail
- Two guest passes per year (share your monthly service with someone)

### Devotee — ₹12,200 / month
- **Two** 60-minute services each month (mix and match massage or facial)
- 20% off all additional services and retail
- Four guest passes per year + priority booking on weekends

## How it works

- **Rollover:** unused monthly services roll over for up to 60 days, so a busy month is
  never wasted.
- **Sharing:** guest passes let a member share their monthly service with a friend or
  partner at the member price.
- **Commitment:** memberships have a 3-month minimum, then continue month-to-month.
- **Pause:** you can freeze your membership for up to 2 months per year (travel, injury,
  etc.) — just let us know before your billing date.
- **Cancel:** after the 3-month minimum, cancel anytime with 10 days' notice before your
  next billing date.
- **Billing:** your card is charged on the same date each month. Any rolled-over credits
  are used before new ones.

## Is a membership worth it?

If you come in even once a month, yes — the monthly service alone is priced below the
walk-in rate, and the member discount applies to add-ons, packages and gift purchases too.
Ask us and we'll help you pick the plan that fits how often you visit.
