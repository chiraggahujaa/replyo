# Riverbend Wellness Spa — Hours & Location

## Opening hours

- **Monday – Friday:** 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM
- **Saturday:** 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM
- **Sunday:** 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM

The last appointment of the day is booked so your full treatment finishes by closing time.
We're closed on Thanksgiving, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day, with reduced hours on some
public holidays — please call ahead around holidays.

## Location

**Riverbend Wellness Spa**
2312 South Lamar Boulevard, Suite 120
Austin, TX 78704

- In the South Lamar district, a short drive from downtown Austin.
- **Parking:** free dedicated lot behind the building, plus street parking on South Lamar.
- **Accessibility:** step-free entrance and an accessible treatment room; let us know if you
  need any accommodations.

## How to book

The fastest way is online at our website, or call/text us during opening hours. For couples
services, packages, or groups of three or more, please call so we can coordinate therapists
and rooms. New guests: remember to arrive 15 minutes early for your first visit.
