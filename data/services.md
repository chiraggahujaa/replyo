# BrightSmile Dental — Services

## General Dentistry
Routine check-ups, cleanings, fillings, and preventive care for the whole family.
We recommend a check-up and cleaning every six months.

## Cosmetic Dentistry
- Teeth whitening (in-clinic and take-home options)
- Composite and porcelain veneers
- Smile makeovers

## Restorative Dentistry
- Tooth-coloured fillings
- Root canal treatment
- Crowns and bridges
- Dental implants to replace missing teeth

## Orthodontics
- Traditional metal braces
- Invisalign clear aligners
- Retainers

## Paediatric Dentistry
We welcome children from age 3. Our team is trained to make young patients feel at ease.

## Emergency Dental Care
Same-day emergency slots are reserved daily for severe pain, swelling, broken teeth,
or knocked-out teeth. Call us as early as possible to secure an emergency slot.
