# Riverbend Wellness Spa — Frequently Asked Questions

**It's my first time at a spa. What should I expect?**
Arrive 15 minutes early, fill out a short health form, and relax in our lounge with tea.
Your therapist will ask about your goals and any problem areas before you begin. You'll
undress to your comfort level in private and lie under a sheet and blanket; you're always
professionally draped. There's no "right" way to do this — we'll guide you.

**What should I wear or bring?**
Just yourself. We provide robes, slippers and everything you need. Wear whatever's
comfortable to arrive in, and leave valuables at home.

**How much should I undress?**
To your comfort level. For massage, most guests undress fully or to underwear; you're always
covered by draping and only the area being worked on is exposed. For facials, you stay
clothed from the waist down.

**Do you take walk-ins?**
When we have availability, yes — but weekends and evenings fill up, so booking ahead is best.

**Can I request a specific therapist, pressure, or gender?**
Absolutely. Tell us your preferences when you book and we'll match you where we can.

**I'm pregnant — can I get a massage?**
Yes, our prenatal massage is available from the second trimester, with your provider's OK.
Please mention your pregnancy when booking so we prepare the right setup.

**Do you have fragrance-free or nut-free options?**
Yes. We offer fragrance-free products and nut-free massage oils — just let us know about any
allergies or sensitivities on your intake form.

**Is gratuity included?**
No, gratuity isn't included in the listed price. It's never expected but always appreciated;
15–20% is customary.

**Do you sell gift cards?**
Yes — any amount, online or in person. They never expire and can be used on any service,
package or membership.

**What's your cancellation policy?**
Please give 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations or no-shows are charged 50% of the service.
See our Policies for full details.

**Can I book for a group, a birthday, or a bridal party?**
We love hosting groups. For three or more guests, please call so we can coordinate rooms and
therapists and talk through package options.

**Do you offer injectables, Botox, or medical treatments?**
No — we're a therapeutic day spa focused on massage, facials and body treatments, not a
medical med-spa.

**Where are you and is there parking?**
We're at 2312 South Lamar Blvd, Suite 120, Austin, TX 78704, with a free parking lot behind
the building.
