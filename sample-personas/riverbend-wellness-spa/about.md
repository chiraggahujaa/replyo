# Riverbend Wellness Spa — About Us

Riverbend Wellness Spa is an independent day spa in South Austin, Texas, dedicated to
unhurried, restorative care. We opened in 2016 with a simple idea: a spa should feel less
like a transaction and more like a genuine reset. No upsell pressure, no rushed
appointments — just skilled, licensed therapists and estheticians who take the time to
understand what your body actually needs that day.

## Our philosophy

- **One guest, full attention.** Every treatment is tailored on the day, not run off a
  script. Tell your therapist you want more pressure, less talking, or a focus on your
  shoulders, and that's exactly what you'll get.
- **Clean, considered products.** We use professional-grade, cruelty-free skincare and
  organic, nut-free massage oils. Fragrance-free options are available for every service.
- **A calm building, on purpose.** Soft lighting, quiet rooms, herbal tea in the lounge,
  and phones kept on silent. The space is designed to slow you down the moment you walk in.

## Our team

Our licensed massage therapists (LMTs) and licensed estheticians each bring 5+ years of
experience across modalities including Swedish, deep tissue, prenatal, sports, and
myofascial work. Skin treatments are led by estheticians trained in HydraFacial and
advanced facial techniques. We're happy to match you with a therapist by specialty,
pressure preference, or gender on request.

## Who we're for

First-timers who feel unsure about spa etiquette, regulars who come in monthly on a
membership, couples celebrating something, and busy people who just need ninety quiet
minutes. Whether it's your first massage or your fiftieth, you're welcome here.
