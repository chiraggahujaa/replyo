# Riverbend Wellness Spa — Policies

We keep our policies simple and fair. If anything here is unclear, just ask — we're happy
to explain.

## Booking & arrival

- Book online, by phone, or in person. A card on file is required to reserve.
- **First visit:** please arrive **15 minutes early** to complete a short health-intake
  form and settle in with tea. Arriving late may shorten your treatment so the next guest
  isn't delayed.
- Walk-ins are welcome when we have space, but booking ahead is strongly recommended,
  especially for weekends and couples services.

## Cancellations & no-shows

- Please give at least **24 hours' notice** to cancel or reschedule.
- Cancellations inside 24 hours, or no-shows, are charged **50% of the service price**.
- For couples and package bookings, the full deposit may be retained for late cancellations.
- Membership monthly services follow the same 24-hour rule; a late-cancelled member service
  is deducted from that month's credit.
- Emergencies happen — talk to us and we'll always try to be reasonable.

## Health & safety

- Please disclose pregnancy, allergies (including nut allergies — ask for our nut-free
  oils), injuries, recent surgery, or medical conditions on your intake form. Some
  treatments have contraindications and we may adapt or recommend against a service for your
  safety.
- Prenatal massage is offered from the second trimester with your provider's OK.
- We are a professional therapeutic spa. Any request for inappropriate services will end the
  appointment immediately, with no refund.

## Gift cards, refunds & retail

- **Gift cards** never expire and are non-refundable for cash.
- **Services** are non-refundable once performed. If you're ever unhappy with a treatment,
  tell us before you leave and we'll make it right.
- **Retail products** may be returned unopened within 14 days for a refund, or exchanged if
  gently used and you had a reaction.

## Comfort & etiquette

- You undress to your comfort level; you are always professionally draped, and only the area
  being worked on is uncovered.
- Please silence phones and keep voices low in treatment areas and the lounge.
- Minimum age is **16** (with a guardian's consent for under-18s); some services are 18+.
- Gratuity is not included in the price and is always appreciated (15–20% is customary).
